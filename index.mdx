TestForge is a full-stack examination management platform built for MSBTE-affiliated institutes. Instead of juggling dozens of spreadsheets, you upload your timetable once and TestForge handles seating configuration, block allocation, supervisor assignment, question paper accounting, and every official MSBTE report — reducing exam preparation from weeks to hours.

## Workflow at a Glance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload the Timetable">
    Upload the MSBTE-provided HTML or Excel timetable. TestForge parses subjects, schemes, dates, and sessions automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import Seating Charts">
    Upload the seating arrangement Excel file from MSBTE. TestForge validates each student against known subjects and flags mismatches for review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allocate Blocks & Assign Staff">
    Run automatic block allocation to seat students across blocks, then assign supervisors and relievers. Print allocation sheets directly from the browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conduct the Exam">
    Use the live dashboard to record attendance, mark absent students, handle copy cases, and account for question paper packets in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate Reports">
    Produce every required MSBTE form (F1–F22) plus TestForge's own summary reports with a single click. Print or download as PDF.
  </Step>
</Steps>
