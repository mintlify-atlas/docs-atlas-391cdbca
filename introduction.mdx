## Who Is TestForge For?

TestForge is designed for **MSBTE exam officers and administrative staff** at polytechnic institutes. If your institute manages end-semester examinations under the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education and you still rely on manual processes, TestForge is built for you.

Typical users include:

- **Exam Center Coordinators** managing block allocation, supervisors, and daily operations
- **Office Staff** generating duty orders, seating statements, and question paper accounting sheets
- **Principals and HODs** who need real-time visibility into examination progress

## Tech Stack

TestForge is a full-stack SaaS platform built with production-grade, battle-tested technologies.

| Layer | Technology |
|---|---|
| **Frontend** | Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript 5 |
| **Backend** | FastAPI, Python 3.11, SQLAlchemy |
| **Database** | PostgreSQL 16, Drizzle ORM |
| **Authentication** | Better Auth (email + Google OAuth) |
| **Payments** | Razorpay (subscriptions, promo codes) |
| **Email** | Resend |
| **UI** | Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Framer Motion |
| **File Processing** | Pandas, OpenPyXL, BeautifulSoup4 |
| **Deployment** | Docker, Docker Compose |

## Examination Workflow at a Glance

TestForge guides exam officers through a structured, linear workflow so nothing falls through the cracks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload Timetable">
    Import your MSBTE timetable from an HTML or Excel file. TestForge parses subjects, sessions, and exam dates automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import Seating Chart">
    Upload student seating data. The platform validates enrollment numbers, maps students to subjects, and flags discrepancies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Students">
    Review and approve the student list. Handle absent students, late additions, and institute-to-institute transfers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allocate Blocks">
    Run intelligent block allocation. Students are distributed across available rooms with floor-wise organization and printable allocation sheets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign Supervisors">
    Assign supervisors and relievers from your staff pool. TestForge tracks availability and generates official duty orders automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate Office Orders">
    Produce all pre-examination paperwork — office orders, question paper indent forms, and control room assignments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conduct Examination">
    Use the live Exam Day Dashboard to track attendance, manage copy cases, and monitor question paper packets in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate MSBTE Reports">
    Export official F1–F22 reports, attendance sheets, supervisor reports, and packet accounting statements ready for submission.
  </Step>
</Steps>
